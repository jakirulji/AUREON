One place for your digital life.

AUREON is being developed as a unified file and cloud-storage workspace for Android and Windows. The goal is to help people organize files, connect supported cloud accounts, and manage backup and synchronization from one consistent experience.

Unified file management

Bring files and folders from supported storage locations into one organized workspace.

Cloud connections

Connect supported cloud providers through their official authorization flows. You remain in control of which accounts you connect.

Backup and sync

Tools for managing backup, transfers and synchronization are part of the product direction and are being implemented in stages.

Development status: AUREON is under active development. Features and supported providers may change before a public release. Do not rely on this development project as the sole copy of important files.
